    The owner's musicmade up: plays while a cart runs

    Keys and the wheel

    Click the screen to give the game the keys. Left and Right are Prev and Next; Up and Down turn the wheel; Enter is Select, Space is Play and Esc is Menu, which pauses unless the game takes Menu as a button.

    On the drawn wheel, drag round the ring to turn it. Click the ring for Menu, Prev, Next or Play by where you click, or hold it half a second to hold that button. The middle is Select.

    ArcadeIDE

    Makes games for the Arcade in PodBox, and is part of it: free software under the GNU General Public License, version 2.

    It is built with these open-source projects:

    • CodeMirror and Lezer, the editor (MIT, Marijn Haverbeke and others)
    • Acorn, which reads the code (MIT)
    • dockview, the panels (MIT)
    • fflate, compression (MIT, Arjun Barrett)
    • Lucide, the icons (ISC)
    • sfxr's sound generators (MIT, Tomas Pettersson)
    • Fusion Pixel, the text font beyond ASCII (SIL Open Font License 1.1, TakWolf)
    • wasi-libc, a few C library routines in the engine (MIT; parts from musl, MIT, and cloudlibc, BSD-2-Clause)
    • esbuild, which builds it (MIT)

    Their licences are in full inside this file, and with every other PodBox credit in its README.
