    Keys and the wheel

    Click the screen to give the game the keys. Left and Right are Prev and Next; Up and Down turn the wheel; Enter is Select, Space is Play and Esc is Menu, which pauses unless the game takes Menu as a button.

    On the drawn wheel, drag round the ring to turn it. Click the ring for Menu, Prev, Next or Play by where you click, or hold it half a second to hold that button. The middle is Select.
