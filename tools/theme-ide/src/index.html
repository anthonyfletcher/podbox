Getting started

Open the folder that holds your theme's .rockbox. Its skins are listed in Files by the screen each draws: Now Playing is the .wps, Menus and status bar the .sbs.

The Player is PodBox itself with your theme on it, and a library of invented music to play. Open the Now Playing skin and the player goes to Now Playing; open the menus skin and it goes to the main menu.

Save (Ctrl+S) and the player loads the theme again. With Update as you type on, it does so as you edit, without saving.

The Outline names each viewport by what it shows. Pick one there or on the player, and the Inspector edits where it sits, its font and its colours; drag it on the player to move it.

Keys and the wheel

Click the player's screen to give it the keys. Up and Down turn the wheel; Left and Right are ◀◀ and ▶▶; Enter is Select, Space is Play and Esc is Menu. Hold Esc for Quick Settings.

On the drawn wheel, drag round the ring to turn it. Click the ring for Menu, ◀◀, ▶▶ or Play by where you click, and hold it for as long as you want the button held. The middle is Select.

Theme IDE

Makes themes for PodBox, and is part of it: free software under the GNU General Public License, version 2. The player is PodBox's iPod Video firmware, built for the browser with Emscripten.

It is built with these open-source projects:

  • CodeMirror, the editor (MIT)
  • dockview, the panels (MIT)
  • Lucide, the icons (ISC)
  • SDL, under the player (zlib)
