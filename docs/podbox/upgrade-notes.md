- **Segregate Audiobooks and Segregate Podcasts** are now Auto or Off, and Auto
  is the default. If you never changed them, they are now Auto: books and
  podcasts are kept out of Music, and the Audiobooks and Podcasts rows appear
  on the main menu as soon as the library holds any.
- **The book index reads every book once more**, the first time this build
  starts, to find each book's series.
- **The art cache files its pictures of people under their names** the first
  time this build starts, rather than under the folder they came from. The
  pictures already made are kept.
- **Podcast episodes with a track number**, and no episode number or date in
  their title, tags or file name, are now ordered by the track number, highest
  first, rather than by when their files arrived. A show where two episodes
  share a track number keeps the old order.
- **The Playback Report reads its log again** the first time it opens, so
  plays of a track that a tagger renamed in its own folder (renumbering
  "03 - Song" to "05 - Song") count under the track as it is now, instead of
  appearing as a second entry.
- **Play goes to Now Playing** from Featured Artists, Album Charts, the
  Audiobooks, Podcasts and Books shelves, and the Documents and Images lists,
  as it already did from the library lists and menus.
- **Hotkey on the Now Playing Screen settings is now Select + Play**, which is
  what it always was: the two buttons pressed together. **Select Action**
  gains **Queue**, to open the queue with a press of Select.
