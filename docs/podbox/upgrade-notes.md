- With **USB Sound Card** on, the click wheel now sets the iPod's volume while
  a computer or phone plays through it.
- **Rescan Books, Documents & Images** (Library > Maintenance) is now
  **Rebuild File Index**. The first start after updating walks the disk once
  more, so the Books, Documents and Images lists may take a minute to settle.
- Ejecting the iPod from a computer now waits for the drive to finish
  writing, which can take a few seconds on an iPod Classic. Wait for the
  computer to say it is safe before unplugging.
- A car or dock that changes shuffle or repeat only for while it is
  connected now puts your setting back when you unplug, instead of keeping
  its own.
- Choosing a track while your queue plays now asks **Play Now** or **New
  Queue**, with Play Now selected. Play Now plays the track at once and keeps
  the rest of the queue; to replace the queue as before, choose New Queue.
- **Menu now goes to the main menu from an album opened from Album Covers,
  Artist Portraits or Go to Album**, as it does in every other list. Left
  still goes back to the covers, or to Now Playing.
- **Spike and Music Quiz are now Arcade games**, on the main menu's
  **Arcade** with Brick Mania. Their old high scores are not carried over,
  and Music Quiz's Quiz Questions settings and Spike's Now Playing settings
  are gone: each game keeps its own options in its pause menu. The Music
  menu's Quiz row and the Now Playing menu's Play Spike are gone with them.
