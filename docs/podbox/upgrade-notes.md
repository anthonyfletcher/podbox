- **Segregate Audiobooks and Segregate Podcasts** are now Auto or Off, and Auto
  is the default. If you never changed them, they are now Auto: books and
  podcasts are kept out of Music, and the Audiobooks and Podcasts rows appear
  on the main menu as soon as the library holds any.
- **The book index reads every book once more**, the first time this build
  starts, to find each book's series.
- **The art cache files its pictures of people under their names** the first
  time this build starts, rather than under the folder they came from. The
  pictures already made are kept.
