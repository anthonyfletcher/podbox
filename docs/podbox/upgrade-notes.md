- With **USB Sound Card** on, the click wheel now sets the iPod's volume while
  a computer or phone plays through it.
- **Rescan Books, Documents & Images** (Library > Maintenance) is now
  **Rebuild File Index**. The first start after updating walks the disk once
  more, so the Books, Documents and Images lists may take a minute to settle.
- Ejecting the iPod from a computer now waits for the drive to finish
  writing, which can take a few seconds on an iPod Classic. Wait for the
  computer to say it is safe before unplugging.
- A car or dock that changes shuffle or repeat only for while it is
  connected now puts your setting back when you unplug, instead of keeping
  its own.
